Install (once, about a minute)

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. On a Mac, double-click it; on Windows, right-click → Extract All.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped watch-sync folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Watch Sync.

Keep the unzipped folder where it is; Chrome loads the extension from it. Chrome may show a "developer mode extensions" notice on startup. That's normal for extensions you install yourself.

Using it

  1. Everyone opens the same title on the same service. (An invite link does this for you.)
  2. One person clicks the Watch Sync icon → Start a party on this tab, then Copy invite link and sends it.
  3. Everyone else opens the link. It opens the same title and joins by itself. (Or click the icon on your tab, type the 6-letter code → Join.) The person who started the party gets an Allow / Deny prompt on the video for each person, and after Allow you'll see "Synced". Up to 8 people can join.
  4. Watch. Play, pause and skipping now happen for everyone. A little message tells you who did what.
  5. To chat or send a reaction, move the mouse and click the 💬 button on the right edge of the video. It works in fullscreen too.

The person who started the party can tick Only I can play, pause and skip in the Watch Sync popup. Then if anyone else plays, pauses or skips, their video jumps straight back to the host's spot. Otherwise everyone has control, and anyone who moves on to another episode brings everyone with them.

When the person who started the party moves on to the next episode, everyone else follows by themselves. On Prime Video and Apple TV+, and when anyone else changes what they're watching, a popup offers to open it instead. If the video ever drifts, the joiner is quietly nudged back in line.

On plans with ads, whoever gets an ad keeps watching it and everyone else pauses with a "Waiting for …'s ad" note. When the last ad ends, everyone carries on together. Ads can't be skipped; they're part of the stream. If the note ever gets stuck, press play and choose Watch anyway.

Buffering works the same way: if someone's video gets stuck loading for more than a second, everyone else pauses with "Waiting for … to buffer" and you all carry on together once it's moving again.
